Privacy Policy

My Dairy Farm (میرا ڈیری فارم) · Android app com.abdul.mydairy
Developer: Abdul Rehman · Effective date: 28 October 2026

My Dairy Farm helps small dairy farmers keep records of their animals, milk, sales, credit (udhaar), workers and expenses. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and a password, used to sign in. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password.
  • Farm records you enter: animals (name, type, price, dates, pregnancy and milking details), births, pregnancy injections, milk records, customers (name, optional phone number, rates), milk sales and payments, workers (name, optional phone number, salary) and their payments, expenses, and health reminders.

Customer and worker names and phone numbers are entered by you. Only add people who are happy for you to keep their details. Phone numbers are used only to open your phone's dialer or WhatsApp when you tap Call or WhatsApp; the app does not send messages itself.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Advertising: the app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interactions to show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Diagnostics: Google Firebase may process technical data (such as IP address and device information) needed to operate sign-in and storage.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

3. How we use information

  • To create your account and keep your farm records available on your devices.
  • To calculate totals, balances, profit and reminders shown in the app.
  • To schedule reminders as notifications on your phone. These are created on the phone itself; no reminder data is sent to any server for this.
  • To show ads that keep the app free.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your farm records for advertising.

4. Where data is stored and who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores your account and farm records on Google's servers. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change your records.
  • A copy of your records is kept on your phone so the app works without internet.
  • Google AdMob receives the advertising data described above.

We share data with no one else, except where required by law.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

Your records are kept until you delete them or delete your account. You can delete any record in the app at any time.

Delete your account: in the app open More → Delete account (below Log out), enter your password and confirm. Your account and all farm records are removed from our servers immediately and the copy on your phone is erased. If you cannot use the app, see how to request deletion.

6. Children

The app is meant for farmers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above.
