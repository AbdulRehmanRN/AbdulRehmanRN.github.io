Delete your My Dairy Farm account

My Dairy Farm (میرا ڈیری فارم) · Developer: Abdul Rehman

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open My Dairy Farm and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out. An internet connection is needed.

Can't open the app?

Email abdulrehmanarain980@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my My Dairy Farm account”. We will confirm and delete the account and its data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address).
  • All farm records: animals, births, pregnancy injections, milk records, customers, sales, payments, udhaar, workers, salary payments, expenses and health reminders.
  • The copy of your records stored on the phone you delete from.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Nothing is kept after deletion.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

See also our Privacy Policy.
